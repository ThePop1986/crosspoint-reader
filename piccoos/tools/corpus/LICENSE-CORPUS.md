# PiccoOS test corpus — licence

The EPUB files produced by `make_corpus.py` (titles, author names, chapter text and
cover artwork) are generated from built-in word lists and simple geometric drawing.
They contain no third-party literary text or images.

To the extent possible under law, the PiccoOS project authors have waived all copyright
and related rights to this generated corpus under **CC0 1.0 Universal**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Exception — cover lettering is rendered (as raster pixels) with the bundled
**DejaVu Serif** fonts. The font files in `fonts/` are NOT CC0; they are distributed
under the Bitstream Vera / DejaVu licence in `fonts/LICENSE-DejaVu.txt`. Rendered
images of text are not font software and carry no font-licence obligations.

Names of people in the corpus are invented combinations of common first names and
surnames; any resemblance to real persons is coincidental.
